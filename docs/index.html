Love the slowed version of Agar Tum Saath Ho?

Songs like this one are what slowed + reverb was made for. Listen to the original below, then make your own version of the songs you love.

"Agar Tum Saath Ho" from the film Tamasha (2015). Sung by Alka Yagnik and Arijit Singh, music by A. R. Rahman, lyrics by Irshad Kamil. Official video © T-Series, embedded from YouTube. This site is not affiliated with T-Series.

Only use music you have the rights to. Slowed versions are for personal listening. A remix of someone else's song still belongs to them, so ask the rights holder before you publish it.

Use it inside Claude Code. Install the plugin with /plugin marketplace add nasimnayan/lofi-audio, then ask Claude to make a lofi version of any song on your computer.
